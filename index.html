How it works

From first message to hanging, in four steps.

  1. Send us a message on WhatsApp or visit the shop.
  2. Choose your fabric, color and style.
  3. We measure your windows and make the curtains.
  4. We install them at your place.

Questions

Do you measure at my home or office?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we will arrange a visit.

Can I get sheer and blackout together?

Yes. Many customers hang a sheer layer and a heavier curtain on a double track.

How long does it take?

It depends on the fabric and size. Ask us on WhatsApp for the time for your order.

Do you make curtains for offices and shops?

Yes, we make curtains for both homes and offices.
